SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

From Love Dive
to Blackhole.

IVE closed out three nights at Hong Kong's AsiaWorld-Arena on September 6, the latest stop on the Show What I Am World Tour, days after the group was named "Hottest International Group" at the 2026 TIMA in the same city. The tour now turns toward its finale — three nights at Seoul's KSPO Dome, October 31 through November 2. It caps a year built on second studio album REVIVE+ and its double title tracks, "BLACKHOLE" and "BANG BANG" — the latter IVE's sixth Perfect All-Kill, more than any other girl group has logged — followed by fourth Japanese EP LUCID DREAM in May, and, this August, "I AM" becoming the group's first music video to pass 400 million YouTube views. This page is a working build of how IVE Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking DIVE — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on this era: REVIVE+ is IVE's second full-length album and carries two title tracks rather than one, plus a solo track for each of the group's six members (Gaeul, Yujin, Rei, Wonyoung, Liz, Leeseo). It arrived a little over four years after the group's December 2021 debut, while the Show What I Am tour that carries it runs through its Seoul finale in early November.

4.0 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"A double-title-track album that spends its runtime shedding the princess image and expanding the worldview — 'BLACKHOLE' is the sound of a group betting it can carry two leads at once, and mostly winning."

REVIVE+ (2026): IVE's second full-length album, and its most structurally ambitious release yet. Twelve tracks split across two title songs — "BANG BANG," pre-released ahead of the album, and the cinematic "BLACKHOLE" — bookend a tracklist that also carves out a solo spotlight for all six members, from Wonyoung's "8" to Yujin's "FORCE." "BLACKHOLE" charted internationally on release and later took first place on Show Champion.

The two-lead-single structure is a genuine risk for a group whose earlier eras built around one clear anthem at a time, but it pays off by giving the album a wider emotional range than a typical mini could — moody and expansive on the title tracks, more personal on the solo cuts.

3.5 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"Six tracks that treat a Japanese EP as more than a victory lap — LUCID DREAM leans into unease rather than polish, and Junji Ito's cover art is the whole thesis in one image."

LUCID DREAM (2026): IVE's fourth Japanese EP, and its most visually distinct release to date. Released May 27 through Sony Music Japan, the six-track set pairs a title track MV (out a day early, May 26) with three Japanese-language originals — a higher original-song ratio than the group's earlier Japanese releases, which leaned more on Korean-track re-recordings.

The cover, illustrated by horror manga artist Junji Ito, sets the tone: this is a moodier, more unsettled companion to REVIVE+'s cinematic scale rather than a rehash of it, and it's the clearest sign yet that IVE's Japanese output is being built as its own lane rather than an afterthought.
